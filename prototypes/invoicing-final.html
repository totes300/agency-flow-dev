Monthly Breakdown rows — retainer monthly (no rollover)

State pill — fixed 3-word dictionary: within budget · over budget · in progress. Never any dynamic data (no 2.0h over, no closes May 31, no paid). Hours specifics live in the Hours column; payment state lives in the Inbox. Status column dropped — the State pill carries the period status (closed = within/over budget; not closed = in progress). One signal, one column. Next-action affordance: the oldest closed-uninvoiced row (March) gets a subtle bg-zinc-50 wash and font-medium month name. Just enough to direct the eye, no chrome. Clickable invoice number: INV-2026-01 ↗ is one link target — the doc number itself is the affordance. Legend at the card footer documents the dot colors (which match the pill colors). Sort at the header lets the user flip between oldest-first / newest-first.

Surface 9 New fields

Settings → Invoicing

Two new org-level fields land here: paymentInstructions + invoiceMessageTemplate.

Invoicing

Defaults for every invoice your agency generates.

INV-

Used as the prefix for every invoice number.

Net 30 days

Auto-fills the due date on new invoices.

16

Editable if you're migrating from another invoicing system.

IBAN HU42 1177 3016 1111 1018 0000 0000 SWIFT: OTPVHUHB Reference: {invoiceNumber} Net 30 — pay by {dueDate}

Renders at the bottom of every invoice document. Plain text or simple Markdown.

Thank you for your business. Reply with any questions or feedback on the work delivered this period.

Seeds the "Message to client" block on every new invoice. Editable per-invoice in the editor. Leave empty to omit by default.

Both fields are optional. Empty payment instructions = block hidden on invoice. Empty message template = each new invoice's Message block starts empty (with a "+ Add a message to client" affordance in draft).
